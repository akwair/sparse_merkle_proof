## Original gates, applied separately to each environment

**H-locality:** For each domain, N, query kind and q in {32,128}, compare the median of the three dictionary/ordinary byte ratios in dispersed and clustered conditions. Every development dispersed-minus-clustered difference must be >=0.10, and every confirmation difference must be >0. There are eight development and eight confirmation comparisons. Require all exact size identities and restored proofs to pass. Cross-host byte equality makes this decision an invariance check on the same fixtures, not independent statistical replication of a byte effect.

**H-lifecycle:** For each of the three zlib representations, N, query kind, pattern and q in {1,8}, use the median of 63 within-host matched Reset/fresh total-time ratios (three bundles x 21 repeats). Every one of the 48 development comparisons must be <=0.90, and every one of the 48 confirmation comparisons must be <1.00. Require identical fresh/Reset frames. q in {32,128} remains descriptive; it cannot replace a failed small-bundle condition.

**Conditional engineering gate:** For each of the 32 development cells and eight alternatives to ordinary, the worst-of-three bundle byte ratio must be <=0.80 and the median of 63 within-host paired mode/ordinary total-time ratios must be <=1.05. An effective alternative additionally requires its corresponding confirmation condition to have worst byte ratio <1.00 and median time ratio <=1.05. Evaluate all 256 alternative conditions and report every failure. Confirmation tests byte direction, not the development 20% reduction magnitude. These engineering gates are descriptive rules, not significance tests.

Preserve all constituent comparisons, including near-threshold values and strict inequalities. Report each environment's own pass/fail result. No pooled gate, majority vote, aggregate portability threshold or favorable-subset replacement is introduced.
