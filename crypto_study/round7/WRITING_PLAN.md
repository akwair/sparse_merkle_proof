# Manuscript revision contract

Target: Concurrency and Computation: Practice and Experience. Article type: empirical research. Language: English. Revise title/abstract/introduction/methods/results/discussion/conclusion around checked results. Python remains the authorized plotting backend.

## Argument to test before drafting

For sparse Merkle proof transport that retains an individual-proof verifier, representation choices depend on request repetition, value size, compressor allocation reuse and execution context. Evidence will combine the preserved three-platform controlled corpus, five same-host repetitions, five synthetic read-only application runs and separately registered allocation probes. The study does not introduce a proof construction or establish the optimum deployed service.

The sentence is a writing plan, not a positive result: each clause must be supported or narrowed after the new analyses. No numeric result is to be written before audited outputs are available.

## Evidence architecture

1. Preserve baseline-screening rationale but shorten chronological implementation details where they obscure the empirical question; keep their full evidence in the archive.
2. Explain interface, formats and exact size accounting before experiments.
3. Give compact original three-platform results without claiming cross-machine causality.
4. Add the concrete read-only snapshot case and separately report codec/service denominators, duplicates and payload sizes.
5. Show all five process outcomes and variability; no strongest-run narrative.
6. Add allocation evidence after the timing observations, distinguishing direct measurements from untested GC/latency mechanisms.
7. Retain the multiproof boundary and synthetic/single-seed/shared-host limits, with no outdated statement that temporal repetition or allocation measurement was absent.

## Comparison to published CCPE work

Use Munhoz et al. (cpe.70582) for the structure of application cases, repeated measurements and explicit cost interpretation; use Figiela et al. (cpe.4792) for question-led empirical evaluation and bounded deployment implications. These are methodological precedents, not same-system performance comparators and not an acceptance guarantee. YCSB supplies workload motivation, not evidence of a production proof-service trace. Add primary sources to the related-work synthesis where they support specific claims.
